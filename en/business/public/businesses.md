# Businesses

The Businesses page is the directory of companies registered on the platform. Each card represents a
business, and opening one shows its identity, contact details and the products it publishes.

Use it to research a supplier, confirm that a partner is registered, or check how your own business
appears to others.

## What this page is about

- Browsing businesses by name or type.
- Opening a business profile.
- Inspecting the products a business sells.
- Editing your own business profile and logo.

## Page layout

| Screen | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Business list | Cards with logo, name, type and location |
| Business profile | Identity, description, contact details and social links |
| Products | The published catalog of that business |
| Edit business | Profile fields and image management for your own business |

## How to use it

### Browse businesses

1. Open **Public > Businesses**, or the businesses link offered from supplier screens.
2. Search by name.
3. Open a card to read the profile.

### Read a profile

The profile shows the business name, type, description, contact channels and published catalog. It is the
same information customers see, minus private data. Use it to decide whether to trade with the business
and to copy contact details into a supplier record.

### Inspect products

Choose the products action to open the catalog of that business. Products are grouped and searchable, and
each entry shows the public price and description. Stock quantities and cost prices are not exposed.

### Edit your own business

1. Open your own business profile and choose edit.
2. Update the name, description, contact details and social links.
3. Upload or remove the logo from the image action.
4. Save.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Shown in the catalog and on documents |
| Description | Public text; keep it factual |
| Contact details | Phone, e-mail and website |
| Logo | Image used on the public profile |
| Social links | Optional public channels |

## What is restricted

- Browsing requires `home` read. Editing requires `settings` write.
- You can only edit the business you are signed in to. Another business's edit route returns an access
  error.
- Image changes require the same settings write capability and accept only uploaded image files.
- The catalog exposes published information only: no employees, accounts, invoices or stock levels of
  other businesses.
- Deleting a business is not possible from this screen.
- Edits to your profile affect every branch, because the business is shared.

## Tips and troubleshooting

- **The business is not listed.** It may be unpublished or registered under a different name.
- **The logo did not change.** Confirm the upload finished before saving; an interrupted upload leaves the
  previous image.
- **The edit action is missing.** You are on another business's profile, or your role lacks settings
  write.
- **Contact details are outdated on a supplier.** Update the supplier record in Purchase > Suppliers; the
  catalog is not the operational source of truth.
