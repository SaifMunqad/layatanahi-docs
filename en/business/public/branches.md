# Public Branches

Branches are where customers meet the business. The public branch directory lists locations across the
platform, shows their public profile and, for a partner you work with, lets you pin the branch so it
appears at the top of your purchase and sale pickers.

This page is about discovery. The operational management of your own branches lives in Store > Branches.

## What this page is about

- Browsing branches by name, business or location.
- Opening a branch profile with its public information.
- Reviewing the products and trade history visible for that branch.
- Pinning a branch for faster selection in other modules.

## Page layout

| Screen | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Branch list | Cards with branch name, business, address and image |
| Branch profile | Identity, address, map location, products and transactions |
| Pin action | Adds or removes the branch from your pinned list |

## How to use it

### Browse branches

1. Open **Public > Branches**.
2. Search by branch name or address.
3. Open a card to see the profile.

### Read a branch profile

The profile groups the public information of the branch:

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Branch identity, address, map and contact details |
| Products | The products the branch publishes |
| Purchases and sales | The trade documents visible to your business |
| Transactions | Payments linked to those documents |

The trade tabs are useful when you already have a relationship with the branch: they show what was bought
and sold between the two businesses, not the branch's private ledger.

### Pin a branch

1. Open a branch profile, or use the pin action on a card.
2. Pin the branch. It appears in the pinned sections of purchase and sale receivers.
3. Unpin it when the relationship ends.

Pinning is personal to the signed-in employee and does not alter the branch.

## What is restricted

- Browsing requires `home` read, which every employee holds.
- Pinning and unpinning are write actions but only affect the current user's shortcuts.
- The profile exposes public information and mutual trade documents. It never shows the other branch's
  accounts, salaries or stock levels.
- You cannot edit another branch from this screen. Your own branch is edited in Store > Branches.
- A branch with no public translations may appear without a readable name or address.
- The trade tabs require the documents to be visible to your business; unrelated branches show empty
  tables.

## Tips and troubleshooting

- **A branch is not listed.** It may belong to an unpublished business, or it is not shared with your
  business.
- **The pin action does nothing.** Pins are per user; reload the target screen to see the change.
- **The profile has no products.** The branch has no published stock, or the products are not shared
  publicly.
- **Trade tables are empty.** There are no documents between your business and that branch, or the
  documents belong to another branch pair.
