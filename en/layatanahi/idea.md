# Idea

**From business data to business intelligence.**

Layatanahi started with a simple idea: make business information *easier to manage, organise, and use*.

## How the idea grew

We first built a web system for managing business data. As we understood real businesses better, we saw that **storing information was only the beginning** — businesses also need to manage sales, purchases, products, customers, inventory, and reporting in **one connected place**.

So the idea expanded:

- Sales connect to **inventory**.
- Purchases connect to **suppliers**.
- Activity becomes **reports**.
- Reports become **insights**.
- Data becomes **better decisions**.

> **Tip:** This is exactly how the modules fit together today — see **[Features](/docs/layatanahi/features)**.

## Our AI vision

We asked: *what if businesses could talk to their own system?*

Layatanahi provides intelligent **AI agents** so people can interact with their data in plain language. Business owners and employees can ask things like:

- *“What were our best-selling products this month?”*
- *“Which products need reordering?”*
- *“Which customers have outstanding payments?”*

The goal is not just to answer, but to help businesses **take action** — with the right permissions and approvals. → [Business Chatbot](/docs/business/chatbot)

## Where we are going

Layatanahi is not *just* a database, a sales system, or an AI assistant. It is the effort to connect all of them into **one intelligent business operating system**:

**Data → Operations → Intelligence → Action → Growth**
