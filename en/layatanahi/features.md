# Features

This is a guided tour of what Layatanahi can do. Each capability links to a **how-to guide** where you can learn the exact steps.

## Businesses that work together

At its core, Layatanahi helps businesses **coordinate** — seeing what partners can supply and what they need, so opportunities for partnership, supply, and service become visible. This reduces duplicate work and builds a more connected business network.

> **Tip:** Explore the public side of the network in **[Public Catalog](/docs/business/public/overview)**.

## Selling & buying

- **Make Sale** — record a customer or branch sale, with cart, discount, and payment in one step. → [Make Sale](/docs/business/sale/sell)
- **Make Purchase** — register goods bought from a supplier. → [Make Purchase](/docs/business/purchase/create)
- **Cart & checkout** — customers buy now or add to cart and pay later. → [Purchase](/docs/user/purchase)

## Request & confirmation

Transactions between businesses use a structured **request → accept → pay → confirm** flow, so both the *goods* and the *payment* are verified before a deal is closed. → [Purchase Requests](/docs/business/purchase/requests)

## Online payment (Hesab Pay)

Layatanahi supports **online payments** through Hesab Pay. A payment is started with an invoice number, the provider returns a secure payment URL, and the result is confirmed by a **signed callback** before the system records it — keeping payments *secure* and *traceable*. → [Payment Requests](/docs/business/finance/payment-requests)

## Inventory

Every branch keeps its own **stock**. Purchases add to it, sales reduce it, and transfers move it between branches. → [Inventory Overview](/docs/business/inventory/overview)

## Finance & taxes

The **Finance** module covers accounts, incomes, expenses, salaries, and partners. Tax types such as **CIT**, **BRT**, and **VAT** are calculated automatically and recorded as expenses — with the exact rules documented in their own guide. → [Taxes](/docs/business/finance/taxes)

> **Note:** Tax rates change with law and configuration, so the **[Taxes](/docs/business/finance/taxes)** guide is the *source of truth* — we do not hard-code percentages here.

## Employees & payroll

A business can send **employment requests**; once accepted, a user becomes an employee. Payroll and salary handling live in Finance. → [Employees](/docs/business/store/employees) · [Salaries](/docs/business/finance/salaries)

## Exchange rates

Currencies are converted to **Afghan Afghani (AFN)** through a live exchange-rate feed and stored for accurate financial calculations. → [Accounts](/docs/business/finance/accounts)

## Notifications

The **notification inbox** delivers operational events — requests, orders, payments — to the right people. *Read* notifications are cleaned up automatically after **7 days**, while unread ones stay until you see them. → [Notifications](/docs/business/notifications)

## Sign-in options

- **Google** and **Facebook** sign-in for quick, familiar access
- **Two-factor authentication** for extra account protection

→ [Profile & Settings](/docs/user/profile)

## Reports & analytics

Every day of business activity turns into **reports** you can review and export (for example to **Excel**). → [Reports](/docs/business/store/reports) · [Analytics](/docs/business/store/analytics)
