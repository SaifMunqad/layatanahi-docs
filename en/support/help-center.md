# Help Center

The **Help Center** is your starting point for getting unstuck. Whether you are *shopping*, managing your *account*, or working the *business* side, this hub points you to the right guide fast.

## Popular topics

- **Order status** — where is my package? → see [Purchase](/docs/user/purchase).
- **Returns & refunds** — how do I send something back? → see [Return Policy](/docs/legal/return-policy).
- **Login & password** — I cannot sign in → see [Profile & Settings](/docs/user/profile).
- **Payments** — a charge looks wrong → contact support with your **order reference**.

## Still need help?

> **Tip:** Before contacting us, keep your **order reference** and a short description of the problem ready — it lets the team help you much faster.

If the guides above don't solve it, [contact the team](/docs/support/contact-team) and we will respond during **Sunday–Thursday, 8:00–16:00 (Afghanistan Time)**.
