# Taxes & Compliance

The Taxes module is built to ensure your business remains compliant with local and national regulations without the need for an external accountant. It automatically calculates taxes (like VAT or Sales Tax) on every transaction based on the rules you define in the global settings.

Tax is calculated at the 'Line Item' level during sales and purchases. This means the system knows exactly how much tax was collected from customers and how much was paid to suppliers. It maintains a 'Tax Liability' ledger that shows you the net amount you owe to the government at any given time.

For international trade, the system handles complex tax scenarios including tax-exempt items and different rates for different product categories. This ensures that you are always collecting the correct amount and never overpaying or under-reporting your tax duties.

To configure your tax rules, visit the 'Global Config' (Admin only) or view your current tax status in the 'Finance > Taxes' section. You can see a live summary of 'Tax Collected' vs 'Tax Paid,' giving you a clear picture of your upcoming tax obligations.
