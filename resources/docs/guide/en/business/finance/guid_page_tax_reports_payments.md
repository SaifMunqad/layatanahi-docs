# Tax Reports & Payments

Tax Reporting is the final step in the compliance lifecycle. Our system generates detailed 'Tax Reports' that can be exported directly for filing with government portals. These reports break down your sales, purchases, and the resulting tax calculations into the exact format required by tax authorities.

The 'Tax Payments' feature allows you to record when you have physically paid your tax bill to the government. When you record a tax payment, the system moves the funds from your bank account to the 'Tax Paid' ledger, settling your liability and keeping your balance sheet clean.

You can generate reports for specific periods—monthly, quarterly, or annually. These reports provide a summary of 'Input Tax' (tax you paid) and 'Output Tax' (tax you collected), calculating the final 'Tax Due.' This automation saves days of manual work and eliminates the risk of calculation errors during filing.

To generate a report, go to 'Finance > Taxes' and select 'Generate Report.' Choose your date range and export the file. Once you pay the government, use the 'Record Payment' button to update your internal books and clear your tax liability.
