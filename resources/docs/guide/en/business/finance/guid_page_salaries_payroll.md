# Salaries & Payroll

The Salaries module automates one of the most complex tasks of business management: payroll. It allows you to define base salaries for every employee and automatically calculates their monthly payouts. The system integrates directly with the attendance and expense modules to handle deductions or bonuses if applicable.

Every month, you can generate 'Salary Slips' with a single click. These slips provide a transparent breakdown of the employee's earnings and any withholdings. Once a salary is marked as 'Paid,' the system automatically creates a credit entry in your chosen cash or bank account and a corresponding debit entry in your 'Salary Expense' ledger.

The payroll system also supports 'Advance Payments.' If an employee needs their salary early, you can record a 'Salary Advance' which the system will automatically deduct from their next full payroll cycle. This ensures that your employee support doesn't create accounting headaches.

To manage payroll, visit the 'Salaries' page. You can view the list of all employees and their current payment status. Use the 'Generate' button at the start of each month to prepare the payroll, and 'Confirm Payment' once the funds have been physically handed over or transferred.
