# Staff Requests (Join Business)

The Staff Requests system handles the 'Join Business' workflow, where external users can request to become employees of your organization. This is a streamlined way to onboard new staff without manual data entry. Instead of you creating an account for them, the candidate finds your business on the platform and submits their profile for your review.

When a user submits a join request, it appears in your 'Pending Requests' dashboard. You can review their professional details, skills, and past experience before deciding to approve or decline the request. This puts you in full control of your hiring process and ensures that only verified individuals gain access to your internal business tools.

Once you approve a request, the user is instantly converted into an 'Employee' of your business. You can then immediately assign them to a specific branch and grant them the necessary permissions to start their work. This automated handoff reduces the administrative burden of hiring and gets your new team members productive faster.

To view pending join requests, navigate to the 'Employee Management' section and look for the 'Incoming Requests' tab. You will be notified whenever a new candidate applies. We recommend reviewing these requests regularly to ensure you don't miss out on top talent looking to join your team.
