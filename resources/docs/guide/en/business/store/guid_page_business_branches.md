# Business Branches

Business Branches allow you to manage the physical footprint of your organization. Whether you have two storefronts in the same city or a hundred locations across the country, Layatanahi treats each one as a distinct entity with its own inventory, staff, and financial records. This allows for precise oversight of your entire multi-location operation.

Each branch can be customized with its own unique details, including a specific name, logo, and operating hours. This is particularly useful if your branches operate under different sub-brands or have different focus areas. The system allows you to manage these varied identities from a single, centralized administrative dashboard.

The Branch system is deeply integrated with the Inventory module. You can see live stock levels for every product on a per-branch basis, allowing you to identify which locations are over-performing and which need more support. You can also generate branch-specific profit and loss statements to audit the financial health of each location independently.

To manage your branches, visit the 'Store > Branches' section. Here you can add new locations, edit current branch details, or temporarily close a location for maintenance. Each branch card provides a quick summary of its current staff count and active inventory value.
