# Audit Logs & Activity Tracking

The Activity Tracking system is a complete, second-by-second audit log of every action taken within the platform. Whether someone adds a new product, changes a price, processes a sale, or logs in from a new device, a permanent record is created. This transparency is the ultimate safeguard for your business data.

Audit logs are essential for security and troubleshooting. If a mistake is made—like an accidental stock adjustment—you can look back at the activities to see exactly who made the change and when. This allows you to resolve discrepancies quickly and provides a powerful deterrent against internal fraud or unauthorized access.

Activities are categorized by type (Financial, Inventory, Security, etc.) making it easy to filter through thousands of entries. You can see the 'Old Value' and 'New Value' for every change, giving you full context of what happened. This level of detail is a standard requirement for businesses operating in regulated industries.

To view your logs, visit 'Store > Activities'. You can search for specific actions or filter by employee name. For large organizations, we recommend that managers review the 'Security' activities daily to monitor for any unusual login patterns or permission changes.
