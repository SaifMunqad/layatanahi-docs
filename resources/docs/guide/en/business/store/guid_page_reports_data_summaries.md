# Reports & Data Summaries

Reports are the primary tool for turning raw data into actionable business intelligence. Our system generates a wide variety of reports, including Sales Summaries, Inventory Turnover, Financial Statements, and Tax Compliance documents. These reports are designed to give you a clear, objective view of your business's performance.

Every report is highly customizable. You can filter by date range, branch, category, or even specific employees. This allows you to drill down into the data to find the root cause of trends. For example, you can generate a report to see exactly which products contributed most to your profit margins last month.

Our reports are built for professional use and can be exported in formats like PDF for presentations or Excel for further data analysis. This ensures that you can always take your data with you for meetings with partners, investors, or tax authorities. The visual summaries included in many reports help you spot trends at a glance.

To access your data, go to 'Store > Reports'. Select the type of report you need, apply your desired filters, and hit 'Generate.' We recommend reviewing your 'Key Performance Indicators' (KPIs) at the end of every week to ensure your business is staying on track toward its goals.
