# Employee Management

The Employee Management module is your central hub for building and managing a high-performing team. It allows you to maintain a digital directory of every staff member, from floor cashiers to regional managers. You can store contact information, emergency details, and contractual documents, ensuring that your HR records are always organized and accessible.

A key feature of this module is Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). You can assign specific 'Roles' to each employee, which dictates exactly what they can see and do within the system. For instance, you can grant a cashier access to the Quick Sell interface but prevent them from seeing sensitive financial reports or changing product prices. This ensures operational security while empowering staff with the tools they need.

The system also tracks employee performance metrics. By linking employees to their specific sales, purchases, and activities, you can see who your top performers are and identify where additional training might be needed. This data-driven approach to management helps you optimize your labor costs and reward excellence within your organization.

To manage your team, go to the 'Employees' section. From here, you can add new staff members, update existing profiles, or deactivate accounts for former employees. Use the 'Permissions' tab within an employee profile to fine-tune their access levels across different branches and modules.
