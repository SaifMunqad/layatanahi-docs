# Contacting Support & Help Desk

Layatanahi offers direct customer assistance through our integrated Support Help Desk. Whether you have inquiries regarding an active order, multi-currency payment settlement, branch stock inquiries, or account management, our team is equipped to assist you.

When submitting a support ticket, provide complete details including order tracking numbers, item descriptions, and screenshots if applicable. Support agents review tickets through the centralized System Support console and respond directly via registered email.

You can also consult self-service guides and frequently asked questions across Discovery, Cart, and Account settings before opening a ticket to resolve common inquiries immediately.
