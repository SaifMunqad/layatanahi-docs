# Cart & Checkout

The Shopping Cart is your personal mission control for upcoming purchases. It is not just a list of items; it is a sophisticated calculator that handles multi-currency conversions, shipment cost estimates, and business-specific inventory checks in real-time. This ensures that the price you see is the price you pay.

As you add items from different businesses, the cart organizes them so you can see your total commitment per seller. You can adjust quantities for each item, or move products to a 'Saved for Later' list if you aren't ready to buy them today. The system will alert you if an item in your cart is running low on stock so you can complete your purchase before it sells out.

The Checkout process is a secure, step-by-step workflow designed to protect your data. You will be asked to confirm your shipment address, choose from available delivery methods, and select your preferred payment gateway. We use end-to-end encryption to ensure that your financial details and personal information are never compromised.

To complete a purchase, click the cart icon in the header, review your items, and hit the 'Proceed to Checkout' button. Follow the on-screen prompts to finalize your order. Once finished, you will receive an instant confirmation email and can track your order status directly from your profile page.

For customers: [Browse products](/discover), choose a variant and quantity, open [your cart](/discover/purchase/cart), select a payment method and provide delivery information, then Confirm checkout to place the order. For businesses: use the Purchase section to choose a supplier and products, enter quantities and prices, select the payment account, and submit the purchase.
