# AI Product Catalog Context Debugger

The '/api/system/support/v1/ai/openrouter/chat/debug/products-xlsx' and '/system/support/debug-products' routes debug the product catalog extraction pipeline utilized by the AI shopping assistant.

They verify that product names, categories, pricing, stock levels, and merchant details are structured and formatted properly for AI model ingestion, ensuring that automated customer recommendations reflect accurate real-time inventory.
