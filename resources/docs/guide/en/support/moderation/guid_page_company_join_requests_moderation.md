# Company Join Requests Moderation

The Company Join Requests module ('/system/support/company/dev-join' and '/business-join' in development) provides a complete moderation suite for prospective merchant onboarding applications.

Support staff can review company registration details, inspect owner identity documents, approve applications to activate merchant dashboards ('/approve'), reject invalid submissions ('/reject'), apply operational restrictions ('/restrict'), or update and remove join records.
