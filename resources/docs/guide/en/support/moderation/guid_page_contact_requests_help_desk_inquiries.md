# Contact Requests & Help Desk Inquiries

The Contact Requests Hub aggregates all inquiries, support tickets, and feedback forms submitted by users across the application.

Staff can review incoming messages, update resolution statuses (Pending, In Progress, Resolved), compose direct email replies to users, and purge resolved tickets.
