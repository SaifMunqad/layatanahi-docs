# Support Subscription Manager

The Subscription Manager module allows support personnel to create, inspect, update, and cancel business subscription tiers and plan assignments under the 'support.access:subscriptions' authorization scope.

It enables support teams to manage feature limits, renew licenses, adjust billing intervals, and audit active SaaS subscriptions for merchants across the platform.
